# Attribution

This skill is an adaptation inspired by the ideas and structure of Matt Pocock's `teach` skill from:

https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/teach

Concepts intentionally retained or extended include:

- a persistent teaching workspace;
- a concrete mission that steers teaching;
- high-trust sources instead of relying on model memory;
- knowledge, skill, and real-world wisdom as distinct learning needs;
- short, focused teaching units;
- immediate feedback loops;
- retrieval practice, spacing, interleaving, and the distinction between immediate fluency and durable storage strength;
- learning records that capture demonstrated understanding rather than mere coverage;
- reusable reference material and a canonical glossary.

Mastery Teach substantially extends that design with:

- a researched competency graph before normal teaching;
- prerequisite-aware unlocking;
- explicit proficiency levels;
- evidence provenance and confidence;
- independent/external assessment mapping;
- delayed retention checks;
- a diagnostic phase;
- curriculum versioning and change logs;
- machine-readable schemas and validation;
- a visual map generated from learner state.
